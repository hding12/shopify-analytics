# Operating model — two portfolios, two objectives

## Own-stock commercialization

Goal: actual non-test customer orders and sustainable contribution margin. Prioritize stock availability, fulfillment, post-discount revenue, variable costs, breakeven CPA and real orders. Purchase attribution remains provisional until linked to identifiable ads and orders.

## Sourced-catalog market discovery

Goal: cheaply discover purchasable category demand before manufacturer bulk buying. Third-party listings diversify the storefront. After a customer purchases, the team can fulfill through another merchant if reliable and legally acceptable. A negative contribution per test order can be acceptable as research expenditure; it is **not** automatically a failed experiment.

A probe still needs reliable supply, truthful product specifications, shipping estimates, policy eligibility, measurable buyer orders and an explicit risk limit. Use a limited advertising experiment **only** when passive market signals or search intent justify the risk.

### Evidence ladder

1. **Interest**: impression, search query, site search, product view — directional signals; data quality and QA contamination must be checked.
2. **Purchase**: independently validated, non-test paid order — demonstrates at least one buyer under the observed price/promo/context.
3. **Replication**: multiple distinct buyers/time points and repeatable fulfillment; evaluate promo sensitivity and manufacturer landed COGS before bulk purchase.

Neither 1 click nor 1 order proves stable demand. “At least 2 customers within 14 days” can be an experiment-specific decision heuristic, not a general statistical proof.

### Distinct unit economics

- Own stock: `contribution_before_ads = paid_revenue - variable_product_cost - tax/payment fees - fulfillment - expected returns`; `breakeven_CPA = contribution_before_ads` under documented assumptions.
- Probe: `cost_per_validated_order = (ad_spend / number_of_validated_orders) + fulfillment_subsidy_or_negative_contribution` when orders > 0. When orders = 0, report ad spend, eligible clicks and confidence limits, not an infinite or fictitious CPA.
- Manufacturer hypothesis: forecast landed cost needed for target margin **separately** from current dropship economics. State whether shipping is included, and never mix wholesale and source-site retail prices.

Budget approval is distinct from launching. Google Ads daily budgets and campaign total budgets have different behavior. Confirm the campaign's real budget resource and units before enabling.
